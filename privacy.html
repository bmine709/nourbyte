Privacy Policy

Magic Paint World · Last updated 4 June 2026 · Effective immediately

No accounts No personal data Designed for Families

Magic Paint World is a coloring game for children ages 4–9. We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information about your child. The only data that ever leaves the device is the device's anonymous Android Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to serve a small in-app banner and an occasional rewarded video.

1. Who we are

Magic Paint World is developed and published by Nour Dev, a small studio based in Cairo, Egypt. You can reach the developer at any time at bmine709@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Magic Paint World itself does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, or any other contact information
  • Photos, microphone audio, or camera footage
  • Location data of any kind
  • Contacts, SMS, or call history
  • Analytics events, crash logs, or device diagnostics
  • Purchase history (there are no in-app purchases)

3. Advertising

Magic Paint World shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: one bottom banner during gameplay, and an optional rewarded video the player can choose to watch in exchange for an extra life.

Because the app participates in Google Play's Designed for Families programme, AdMob is configured with:

  • Child-directed treatment enabled — complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • No behavioural or interest-based targeting — ad selection is contextual only.
  • Maximum content rating: G — suitable for general audiences.

The only data point AdMob receives is the device's Android Advertising ID, a randomly generated identifier that users can reset or opt out of at any time in their Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Children's privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Magic Paint World is designed primarily for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from any child. There is no account creation, no chat, no social network integration, no links opening external browsers, and no way for a child to enter personal information inside the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact bmine709@gmail.com and we will take steps to remove it.

6. Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — required to load ads from Google AdMob. The app itself works fully offline once installed.
  • Access network state — lets AdMob check whether the device has connectivity before requesting an ad.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

7. Data retention & deletion

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. Uninstalling the app removes every trace of your local game progress.

8. Third-party services

The app integrates exactly one third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — ad serving.
    Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data is collected or how it is used, we will publish a new version of this page with a new "Last updated" date at the top and, where required by law, prompt users for fresh consent before the new policy takes effect.
